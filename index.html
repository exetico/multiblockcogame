THE FIELD GUIDE

A shared space.
A shared win.

Fill complete horizontal rows across the entire 12 × 12 field. Each player has a seven-column reach; the two glowing center columns belong to both of you. Pieces cannot pass through each other. If either side tops out, the round ends for the team.

01 / Park it

There’s one shared park slot. Press C / . to store your piece or take the parked one. Once per active piece, until you lock it.

02 / Pass it

Press V / , to request a trade; your partner presses theirs to confirm. Both pieces respawn on their new side. Press again to cancel.

03 / Relay pieces

Every tenth piece per player is marked ⇄. It must change hands before it can lock. Trade it, or park it for your partner. Optional before a new game.

04 / Make it count

Clear 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 rows at once for 100 / 300 / 600 / 1,000 XP. Score also rewards spins, combos, back-to-back big clears, and drops. Every ten rows raises the level.
