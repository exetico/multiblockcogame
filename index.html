THE FIELD GUIDE

A shared space.
A shared win.

Fill complete horizontal rows across the entire 12 × 12 field. Each player has a seven-column reach; the two glowing center columns belong to both of you. Pieces cannot pass through each other. If either side tops out, the round ends for the team.

01 / Park it

There’s one shared park slot. Press C / R CTRL to store your piece or take the parked one. Once per active piece, until you lock it.

02 / Pass it

Press V / BACKSPACE to request a trade; your partner presses theirs to confirm. Both pieces respawn on their new side. Press again to cancel.

03 / Relay pieces

Every tenth piece per player is marked ⇄. It must change hands before it can lock. Trade it, or park it for your partner. Optional before a new game.

04 / Make it count

Clear 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 rows at once for 100 / 300 / 600 / 1,000 XP. Score also rewards spins, combos, back-to-back big clears, and drops. Every ten rows raises the level.

Left player: A S D move, W / Q rotate, SPACE drops. Right player: ← ↓ → move, ↑ / R SHIFT rotate, ENTER drops. Keys are physical positions, so they work on Danish and other layouts. Change any key or controller button under CONTROLS in the footer.

Controllers welcome.

Connect a standard controller and press a button. The first controller plays right; the second plays left. Keyboards stay active.

Ghost outlines show the landing position. Escape pauses. Use FOCUS for a larger field and the gear for display settings. Sound is synthesized on your device. Best score and lifetime team XP stay in this browser. A keyboard or controller is required; touch controls are not included.

Use Pause in the header centre, Escape, or your controller's pause button. New Game pauses first and asks twice before discarding an unfinished round. The memory-card icon opens three save slots. Progressive speed is on by default: every ten cleared rows speeds up falling, not elapsed time. Turn it off in settings for a steady pace; levels, score and XP still advance. Settings can hide the header and footer. With the header hidden, floating menu and pause buttons remain available.
