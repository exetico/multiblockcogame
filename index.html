More ways to tune your game

Display & sound

The gear controls view, grid lines, hints and header/footer visibility. Fullscreen is separate from view mode. The music-note button toggles locally synthesized effects; there is no background music.

Speed & relays

Progressive speed is on: falling gets faster every ten cleared rows, not with time. Turn it off in settings for a steady pace. Hand relay pieces to your partner before lock. A missed handoff locks normally with a miss notice, never a freeze. You can disable relays before a new round. Neither choice changes row-based XP.

Controllers

Connect by USB, or pair in your device's Bluetooth settings, then press a button here. The first detected controller plays right, the second left. Click a sidebar's device label for setup and side switching. The browser cannot pair controllers itself.

Keep a stage

Restore the header with ☰ and use the memory-card icon for three browser-local save slots. Loads stay paused. Restore the footer through settings for New Game, which asks twice before replacing an unfinished round. Clearing site data removes saves.

View and settings are remembered in this browser using local storage and a preference cookie. Escape, × or Continue keeps the current view; new visitors start in Immersive. Reopen this introduction from settings or How to play at any time.

THE FIELD GUIDE

A shared space.
A shared win.

Fill complete horizontal rows across the entire 12 × 12 field. Each player has a seven-column reach; the two glowing center columns belong to both of you. Falling pieces can pass through each other; settled blocks are solid. If either side tops out, the round ends for the team.

01 / Park it

There’s one shared park slot. Press C / K to store your piece or take the parked one. After parking, lock or complete a trade before parking again. You can park the piece received through a confirmed trade.

02 / Pass it

Press V / L to request a trade; your partner presses theirs to confirm. Both pieces respawn on their new side. Press again to cancel.

03 / Relay pieces

Every tenth piece per player is marked ⇄. Trade it or park it for your partner before lock. If the handoff is missed, it locks normally with a miss notice and gives you the next piece. Hard drop locks immediately. Optional before a new game.

04 / Make it count

Clear 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 rows at once for 100 / 300 / 600 / 1,000 XP. Score also rewards spins, combos, back-to-back big clears, and drops. Every ten rows raises the level.

Pieces have a 500 ms lock window on the floor or settled stack. Successful movement/rotation refreshes it up to fifteen times, including square-piece rotation. A crowded spawn can be moved or rotated to safety; the round ends only when a piece locks above the board. New pieces start with a fresh gravity interval.

Falling pieces can overlap in the shared columns, including their landing outlines. First to lock gets the cells, not first to move there. The other falling piece moves up just enough to sit above the newly settled blocks, keeping its horizontal position and rotation.

Left player: A S D move, E / Q rotate, W drops. Right player: ← ↓ → move, . / , rotate, ↑ / ENTER drops. Keys are physical positions, so they work on Danish and other layouts. Change any key or controller button under CONTROLS in the footer or through the gear.

Controllers welcome.

Connect a standard controller and press a button. The first controller plays right; the second plays left. Keyboards stay active.

Ghost outlines show the landing position. Escape pauses. Use FOCUS for a larger field and the gear for display settings. Sound is synthesized on your device. Best score and lifetime team XP stay in this browser. A keyboard or controller is required; touch controls are not included.

Use Pause in the header centre, Escape, or your controller's pause button. New Game pauses first and asks twice before discarding an unfinished round. The memory-card icon opens three save slots.
